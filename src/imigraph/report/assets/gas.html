The model. $particles disks move in straight lines. When two disks overlap while approaching each other, the velocity components along the line of centres are exchanged (an elastic collision of equal masses), which conserves momentum and kinetic energy exactly; the overlap is then removed. Walls are specular: the velocity component normal to the wall flips, so every disk keeps its speed. Positions advance with a fixed time step, so a collision happens at the first step in which two disks overlap, which shifts it by at most one step and never changes the energy.

The experiment. All disks start on a grid in the left half of the box with the same speed in random directions, a state far from equilibrium. The gas expands into the empty half (entropy rises), disks collide ($collisions collisions in this run) and the speeds relax to the two-dimensional Maxwell–Boltzmann law, whose only parameter follows from the conserved energy: σ² = E / (n m).
